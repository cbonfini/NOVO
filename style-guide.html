Brand System
NOVOUNIVERSITY

The identity of a university built for the next evolution of thinking — where learning evolves with AI and real good work builds the awareness that helps whole systems thrive.

The Wordmark

NOVO is a pure wordmark — set in EB Garamond, all caps, with generous letter-spacing. There is no symbol or icon. The word itself is the mark. "UNIVERSITY" sits beneath in spaced General Sans. Never add a graphic, flame, or ornament.

Do

  • Keep the Garamond letterforms and wide tracking
  • Use on royal ink, deep royal, or ivory backgrounds
  • Give it clear space — never crowd it
  • Scale proportionally

Don't

  • Add a symbol, flame, or icon
  • Recolor the wordmark outside the palette
  • Condense, stretch, or re-letter it
  • Set it in a different typeface

Color

Deep Royal is the primary — regal, serious, timeless. Forest green is the complement across the color wheel — living, growing, renewing. Gold is the accent of achievement and light. Ivory and silver carry the neutrals.

Typography

Two typefaces. EB Garamond (serif) carries the voice — headlines, the wordmark, editorial moments, and italic emphasis. General Sans (Fontshare) carries the function — body copy, labels, navigation, and buttons.

A New Renaissance of Thinking

Voice

Alchemy — a renaissance with an AI kick. We speak with the gravity of a great institution and the warmth of a human invitation. Elevated but never cold; hopeful but never naive.

Elevated

Classical, literary cadence. We borrow the dignity of the Renaissance and the university's oldest purpose.

Human

Warm, direct, second-person. We invite ("Begin your Opus"), we don't sell. Kindness and compassion are the point.

Purposeful

Every line points to real good in the world. We speak of impact, contribution, and stewardship — not credentials.

Forward

We stand at the threshold of what's next — technology in concert with the human difference, for good.

Say

  • The next evolution of thinking
  • Pay with Time, Talent, or Tithes
  • Your Opus · Living Works
  • Contribute meaningful good
  • Be part of the change for good

Avoid

  • "Free" / "tuition-free"
  • Corporate or salesy language
  • Credits, seat time, transcripts as the goal
  • Hype without substance

Signature Vocabulary

The proprietary language of the NOVO model. Use these terms consistently and capitalized.

Opus

A learner's cumulative Great Work — the true substance of their degree.

Living Works

The individual real-world projects that make up an Opus.

Time · Talent · Tithes

The three ways a learner contributes in exchange for their education.

The Four Voices

Faculty, peers, community, and impact — who assess every Living Work.

The Six Movements

Encounter · Foundation · Apprenticeship · Intervention · Evidence · Stewardship.

The Next Evolution of Thinking

The promise and rallying cry of the university.
