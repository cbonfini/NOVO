How to Begin

Four steps to your first Living Work.

There's no gate and no test score — only your willingness to learn, to do good, and to contribute in the way that fits your life. Tell us who you are and how you'll give back, and we'll help you begin.

  1. 1

    Introduce yourself

    Share who you are and the good you feel called to do.

  2. 2

    Choose how you'll contribute

    Time, Talent, or Tithes — the exchange that fits your life.

  3. 3

    Meet your community

    We match you with mentors and peers in your field.

  4. 4

    Begin your Opus

    Start your first Living Work — and begin building work that matters.

Welcome to the Encounter.

Thank you for beginning. This is a demonstration form — in the live university, a mentor would reach out within days to help you shape your first Living Work. The change for good starts now.

"An education should leave the learner — and the world — measurably better."

That is the whole model: open resources, real projects, evidence of impact, and honest engagement with the technologies reshaping our age. You don't have to watch it happen — you can help build it.
