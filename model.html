The Model

Learning that makes real positive impact you can show.

Cost should never be the barrier to a great education. At NOVO, instruction is built on open, world-class resources — and every learner gives back, so the university sustains itself and the world is left measurably better.

Three Ways to Contribute

You pay with what you have.

Time, Talent, or Tithes. Every learner chooses how they give back — and every path keeps NOVO alive for the next.

I
Time

Build good where you live.

If you contribute Time, you give back to your community through a project you design and develop over the course of your degree. It is real, local good — a garden, a tutoring circle, a clean-water fix, a small enterprise — carried out where you live and measured by the difference it makes.

II
Talent

Do good work for the university.

If you contribute Talent, you put your skills to work for NOVO itself — building tools, teaching peers, designing curriculum, translating materials, mentoring newcomers, engineering the platforms that carry the university forward. Your gift is your craft.

III
Tithes

Keep the door open for others.

If you contribute Tithes, you pay a modest assessment — a tithe — that sustains the university and keeps instruction open for the learners who come after you. It is not tuition; it is stewardship of a shared inheritance.

Rigor, Reimagined

Assessed by many voices.

Real work is judged by more than a single grade. Every Living Work is reviewed from four directions.

Voice I

Faculty

Scholars assess the intellectual rigor, depth, and integrity of your work.

Voice II

Peers

Fellow learners assess the craft, clarity, and honesty of what you made.

Voice III

Community

The partners you served assess whether it was genuinely useful to them.

Voice IV

Impact

The evidence itself — the measurable good your work left in the world.

The Currency of the Degree

Living Works become your Opus.

You don't accumulate credits. You complete Living Works — real projects, each reviewed by the four voices, each leaving the world better than it was.

Sequenced together, your Living Works form your Opus: a cumulative Great Work that is the true substance of your degree, and the proof of who you have become.
