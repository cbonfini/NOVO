The university has always had a social purpose. We are restoring it — a place where learners contribute meaningful good to the world while they learn, evolve with the technologies reshaping our age, and discover their purpose.

The Model

Learning that makes real positive impact you can show.

Instruction is built on open, world-class resources, so cost is never the barrier. But a NOVO education is never simply given. Every learner contributes in one of three ways — so the university sustains itself and the world is left better for it.

Contribution I

Time

Give back to your community through a project you develop across your degree — real good, delivered where you live.

Contribution II

Talent

Put your skills to work doing good work for the university itself — building, teaching, designing, translating, mentoring.

Contribution III

Tithes

Pay a modest assessment — a tithe — that keeps the university strong and instruction open for the learners who follow.

Rigor, Reimagined

Assessed by many voices — not one grade.

Your work is judged the way real work is judged in the world: by faculty for its rigor, by peers for its craft, by community partners for its usefulness, and by the evidence of the impact it actually makes.

No hollow credits. A NOVO degree is earned through a sequence of Living Works — each one real, each one reviewed, each one leaving the world measurably better.

AI at NOVO

Taking AI seriously — as a tool, a collaborator, and an open question.

Every NOVO learner works with an AI co-mentor built for their field — always paired with human faculty, and always with humans holding the judgment. We prepare people to work well with the technologies reshaping their fields, and to question them honestly.

Through our Global AI/Human for Prosperity Research Hub, the university studies how humans and increasingly capable AI systems can coexist well — including rigorous, open-minded research on the possible moral status and welfare of AI systems, and what would follow if they matter.
