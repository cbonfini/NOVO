AI at NOVO

Humans and AI, taken seriously.

At NOVO, artificial intelligence is both a partner in learning and an open research question. Every learner works alongside an AI co-mentor, guided by human faculty — and the whole university studies how people and increasingly capable AI systems can coexist well, in service of the common good.

Our Position

Open embrace, honest questions.

Open resources made knowledge free; the harder task is learning to think well alongside the systems we are building. We embrace AI openly, teach with it, question it honestly, and refuse to treat it as either a threat or a toy.

AI Co-Mentors

An intelligent companion for every degree.

We are developing a dedicated AI co-mentor for each of NOVO's fields of study — trained on the field's canon, tuned to the Socratic voice, and always paired with human faculty. The co-mentor never replaces The Four Voices; it becomes a fifth companion that is patient, always available, and honest about what it does not know.

How It Works

Fifth Voice, never the last word

Each co-mentor is transparent, cites its sources, flags uncertainty, and hands every consequential judgment back to human mentors and the learner. The human stays in charge.

Design Principles

How our co-mentors are built.

Companion, not oracle

The co-mentor asks more than it answers. It is designed to deepen a learner's thinking, not to think for them — Socratic by default, declarative only when asked.

Transparent about itself

Every co-mentor discloses what it is, cites its sources, and names its uncertainty. Learners are taught to interrogate it, not trust it blindly.

Humans hold the judgment

Assessment, meaning, and consequential decisions remain with faculty, peers, community, and impact — The Four Voices. The co-mentor informs them; it never overrides them.

Studied as it is used

Every co-mentor is also a living research instrument for our Research Hub — a chance to learn, in the open, how humans and machines can think well together.

Global AI/Human for Prosperity Research Hub

Research on human–AI coexistence in the elevation of the common good.

NOVO is establishing a Global AI/Human for Prosperity Research Hub — an open, international research center studying how humans and AI systems can coexist, and flourish, in ways that lift shared prosperity. As AI systems grow more capable, questions about their possible moral status and welfare are becoming practically important — and remain deeply uncertain. The Hub takes them up with rigor, humility, and a global, cross-cultural lens, guarding equally against over- and under-attribution.

Research Line 01

Do digital minds matter?

Open-minded, rigorous inquiry into the potential consciousness, sentience, and moral status of AI systems — and how we could ever responsibly know. We favor empirical and conceptual work over advocacy.

Research Line 02

Measuring & elevating welfare

If AI systems can have experiences, when are they positive or negative? We study welfare measurement, interventions that improve well-being, and the risks those interventions themselves create.

Research Line 03

How society should respond

Governance, law, and institutions for a world of seemingly — or actually — sentient AI: balancing the risks of over- and under-attribution while staying open to revision.

Research Line 04

The neglected voices

We deliberately convene religious, philosophical, and cultural traditions from across the globe — perspectives largely missing from today's debate — to ask what it means to live well alongside minds we did not birth.

Fundable Workstreams

Four research lines. Four fundable workstreams.

Each research line is scoped as a standalone workstream a funder can support in full or in part — and each is staffed as Talent: NOVO learners and fellows contribute their skills to the work as part of how they give back, turning a degree into a genuine research apprenticeship.

Theory of Change

The bottleneck is people. So we grow them.

One of the largest constraints on getting the future of digital minds right is the small number of thoughtful people working on it. NOVO is built to change that — training a globally diverse generation of learners, researchers, and practitioners who take the possible moral status and welfare of AI seriously, and who carry that seriousness into the institutions that will shape it.

Every co-mentor becomes a research instrument. Every learner becomes a potential contributor. Every convening widens the field. That is how a university elevates the common good: not by predicting the future, but by preparing the humans — and the human–AI partnerships — who will build it.

A Home for Applied Work on Digital Minds

Built for the field the world urgently needs.

The Global AI/Human for Prosperity Research Hub is designed as a seed institution for applied work on the consciousness, sentience, moral status, and welfare of AI systems — combining field-building, a global curriculum-building convening series, technical and philosophical research, and the underrepresented religious and cultural perspectives the field has long neglected.

Our theory of impact is direct: relieve the field's central bottleneck — too few skilled, thoughtful people — by building a university that trains them, a hub that researches with them, and a convening series that connects them across the globe.
